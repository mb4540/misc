Understanding Jemba9's business, its Quality Management System scope, and who the QMS serves

Student Introduction

Every Jemba9 employee works inside a Quality Management System, whether they realize it or not. This topic is the front door to that system: it explains what Jemba9 does, what the QMS covers, and whose needs it exists to satisfy.

Jemba9 is an aerospace and defense company whose mission is "accelerating what's next in space." Rather than building every space product from scratch, Jemba9 uses a proprietary process — Spacify™ — to adapt proven commercial computers, controllers, data routers, power supplies, robotics, and sensors into space-qualified hardware, at roughly half the time and cost of a traditional ground-up space program. The company also runs J9CM, an in-house PCB assembly operation.

That business model is exactly why AS9100D matters here: Jemba9 is promising customers — often government and prime-contractor customers under ITAR/EAR — that a commercially-sourced product can be trusted on an orbital mission. The QMS is the evidence that Jemba9 controls that adaptation process with the same rigor as a traditional space hardware manufacturer, from design and supplier qualification through production, inspection, and delivery.

Key facts every employee should know cold:

Headquarters
Longmont, Colorado
CAGE Code
9TVN7
Primary standard
AS9100D (9100:2016)
Export controls
ITAR/EAR apply to all applicable documents; only authorized persons may access them
QMS scope
Design, development, manufacture, assembly, test, and delivery of aerospace/defense products and services, plus program management, supply chain, and customer support
Clause 8.5.3 (customer property)
Applies only when a customer supplies customer-furnished equipment (CFE) or government-furnished equipment (GFE); when it does, QP-008 governs handling

Who needs this training: Everyone. This is the foundational session that every new hire completes before any role-specific QMS training, and the one section of the Quality Manual every employee is expected to be able to summarize in their own words.

Syllabus & Training Goals

Class Syllabus

  1. Jemba9's business: the Spacify™ model, product lines, and why "space-qualifying" commercial technology requires a rigorous QMS
  2. What AS9100D is and how it relates to ISO 9001:2015 (it incorporates it, plus aerospace-specific requirements)
  3. The QMS scope statement — what activities and locations are in scope, including remote/home-office personnel
  4. Permissible exclusions and why Jemba9 currently excludes none permanently (clause 8.5.3 example: CFE/GFE)
  5. Interested parties: government/DoD/NASA customers, commercial aerospace customers, employees, investors, suppliers, regulators, the AS9100 registrar, and IAQG/OASIS — and what each one needs from Jemba9
  6. Internal and external context: rapid growth, government contracting discipline, space hardware's high-consequence quality bar, LEAN culture, Cetec ERP, and SharePoint as the document backbone
  7. The four QMS process groups — Leadership, Core Value Stream, Supporting, Quality — and how they interact
  8. Where to find the controlled documents and how this training program maps to them

Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  • Describe Jemba9's business model and explain in one sentence why AS9100D certification matters to it
  • State the QMS scope statement and name at least one permissible exclusion and its condition (CFE/GFE)
  • Name at least four interested parties and one expectation each
  • Identify which of the four process groups their own job function belongs to
  • Locate the Quality Manual (QM-001) and Process Architecture (QM-ARCH-001) in the QMS repository/SharePoint library
  • Explain Jemba9's ITAR/EAR posture and what "authorized person" means for document access

Recommended audience:

All employees (mandatory) New hire onboarding

Suggested periodicity: Once at onboarding; refresher at annual QMS awareness training.
