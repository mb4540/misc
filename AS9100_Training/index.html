AS9100D Rev D Quality Management System — Employee Training Curriculum

Purpose of This Site

This training portal breaks the Jemba9 Quality Management System (QMS) — built on the AS9100D standard — into 13 role-relevant training topics, one per QMS process area. It responds directly to Paul Rutt's request (see internal correspondence, 2026-07-21) to identify key training topics within each area of QMS documentation before building a training matrix by job function, in-person training sessions, and eventually a self-service training portal with assessments.

Each topic page provides:

  1. A header section identifying the exact standard, manual, policy, procedure, and template documents the topic is drawn from — with AS9100D clause references and repository file paths.
  2. A student introduction explaining the topic in plain language and why it matters at Jemba9 specifically.
  3. A syllabus and training goals section outlining what the class will cover and what participants must be able to do afterward.
  4. A reviewer notes section so Paul, the Quality Manager, or any subject-matter reviewer can record feedback directly on the page tied to its source documents (see "How to Capture Notes" below).

Training Program Structure

The 13 topics are organized using Jemba9's own four QMS process groups (Quality Manual §7, Process Architecture QM-ARCH-001), so the training program mirrors how the company already thinks about its operations.

Leadership Processes

  1. QMS Overview & Company ContextCompany scope, org context, interested parties, process architecture
  2. Quality Policy & ObjectivesPolicy commitments and the 10 measurable Quality Objectives
  3. Management ReviewQuarterly leadership review of QMS performance

Quality System Processes

  1. Risk ManagementRisk registers, program risk, risk-based thinking
  2. Nonconformance & CAPANCR/NMR, corrective action requests, root cause
  3. Internal AuditInternal audit program and reporting

Core Value Stream Processes

  1. Design & DevelopmentSpacify adaptation, design reviews, verification & validation
  2. Supplier ManagementSupplier qualification, ASL, incoming inspection
  3. Counterfeit Parts PreventionDetecting and preventing counterfeit electronic parts
  4. Production & InspectionManufacturing control, FAI, calibration, product safety

Supporting Processes

  1. Document ControlNumbering scheme, revisions, approvals, review notes workflow
  2. Configuration ManagementBaselines, ECOs/ECRs, change control
  3. Training & CompetencyCompetency matrix, training records — the subject of this program itself

Document Coverage

These 13 topics cover all 37 controlled documents in the Jemba9 QMS Document Register: 6 Level 1 documents (manual, policies, objectives), 13 Level 2 procedures, and 18 Level 4 templates/forms. Each topic page's header table lists exactly which documents it draws from.
