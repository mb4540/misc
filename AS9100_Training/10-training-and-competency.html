Competency, awareness, and the training record that makes this entire training program auditable

Student Introduction

This topic is different from the other twelve: it is not just a subject to be trained on, it is the record-keeping mechanism for the entire Jemba9 QMS Training Program. Every other topic page in this site — QMS Overview, Risk Management, Design & Development, and so on — ultimately produces one line item on an employee's Training Record / Competency Matrix (QF-010).

This is also the exact question Paul Rutt raised in his July 21, 2026 email: Jemba9 needs "a training matrix for each of the employee roles within Jemba9 (e.g. assembly technician, design engineer, supply chain, etc.)," a required-training-periodicity table (e.g., ESD training annually for assembly technicians vs. once every three years for supply chain), and eventually a way to score tests and record pass/fail results per employee. QF-010 is the template that record will live in; the "QMS Role-Based Training Requirements" table inside it already sketches a first draft of that matrix, mapping each procedure to the roles who need it.

AS9100D requires two related things here: competence (clause 7.2 — people doing work that affects quality must be competent, and Jemba9 must evaluate the effectiveness of training, not just attendance) and awareness (clause 7.3 — every employee must understand the quality policy, their contribution to it, and the implications of not conforming). QF-010's "Effectiveness Evaluation" section (post-training test score ≥ 80%, OJT observation, or quality metric trend) is exactly how Jemba9 satisfies the "not just attendance" requirement — and it is the design point Paul asked about directly: how tests get scored and how pass/fail is recorded.

Who needs this training: HR Lead and Quality Manager (as record owners/administrators), all people managers (who sign off on their reports' competency), and every new hire during onboarding (so they understand what is being tracked about their own training and why).

Syllabus & Training Goals

Class Syllabus

  1. Why AS9100D treats training as a quality control, not an HR formality (clauses 7.2 Competence, 7.3 Awareness)
  2. Walkthrough of QF-010: Employee Information, QMS Role-Based Training Requirements table, Competency Assessment, Certifications, Effectiveness Evaluation, and Acknowledgment sections
  3. How the 13 topics in this training program map onto QF-010's "Training Topic" rows (this program is the syllabus behind that table)
  4. Building a role-based training matrix: job function × required topic × periodicity (Paul Rutt's request) — e.g., ESD handling annually for production/receiving vs. multi-year cycles for less-exposed roles
  5. Effectiveness evaluation methods: post-training test scoring, on-the-job observation, and quality-metric trending — and what "pass" should mean before a retake is required
  6. Record retention: duration of employment plus 7 years; update within 5 business days of completion or assessment
  7. Status of open reviewer notes on QF-010 (see pending-note box above) and how document owners action or dismiss review notes per docs/review-notes-guide.md

Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  • Explain the difference between AS9100D "competence" (7.2) and "awareness" (7.3) and give a Jemba9 example of each
  • Complete every section of QF-010 for a hypothetical new hire, including both hire-date fields once the open reviewer note is actioned
  • Identify which QMS training topics are mandatory for their own department using the QMS Role-Based Training Requirements table
  • Describe at least two acceptable effectiveness-evaluation methods and Jemba9's target pass threshold once defined
  • Locate and read an open reviewer note on the qms-review-notes branch and describe how the document owner should action or dismiss it

Recommended audience:

Quality Manager HR Lead People managers All employees (onboarding overview)

Suggested periodicity: Quality Manager/HR Lead: at QF-010 revision; people managers: annually alongside QO-09 Training Completion review; all employees: once at onboarding.
